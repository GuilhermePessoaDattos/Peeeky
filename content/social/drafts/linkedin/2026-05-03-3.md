**Date:** 2026-05-03
**Type:** Practical Insight (Document follow-up timing)

---

The best time to follow up on a proposal is not Monday morning.

It's within 2 hours of them opening it.

Here's why this works:

- The document is literally in front of them. It's top of mind.
- You're responding to intent, not guessing at availability.
- A well-timed follow-up feels like a coincidence, not pressure.

What to say when you follow up immediately after a view:

→ Don't: "Just checking in on the proposal I sent."
→ Do: "Happy to answer any questions on the pricing section" (if you saw them spend time there)
→ Do: "Noticed you had a chance to look at it — want to set up 20 minutes this week?"

The second message is better not because it's more clever. It's better because it's specific and timed.

Most sales reps follow up 4-7 days after sending. The ones who follow up within the first 2 hours of a view close more.

You don't need a complex CRM to do this. You need to know when your document was opened.
