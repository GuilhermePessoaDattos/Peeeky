# LinkedIn Post — Building in Public #1
**Date:** 2026-05-03
**Type:** Building in Public (Metrics)

---

We hit 500 active workspaces on Peeeky last week.

Here's what 6 months of building in public actually looked like — the numbers, not the vibes:

- Month 1: 11 signups. All friends.
- Month 2: 38 signups. First strangers.
- Month 3: 94 signups. First paid plan conversion.
- Month 4: 178 signups. Word of mouth kicked in.
- Month 5: 312 signups. First churn lesson.
- Month 6: 500+ active workspaces.

The hardest part wasn't building. It was resisting the urge to rebuild when growth was slow.

What actually moved the needle: being specific about the problem we solve. "Track your documents" didn't land. "Know which slide investors spent the most time on" did.

If you're early-stage and growth feels slow — we're probably two months behind where I thought we'd be. That's normal. Keep shipping.
