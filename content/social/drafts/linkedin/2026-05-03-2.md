# LinkedIn Post — Building in Public #2
**Date:** 2026-05-03
**Type:** Building in Public (Lessons)

---

We killed a feature that took us 3 weeks to build.

We launched "document chat" — let recipients ask AI questions about your PDF. People loved it in demos. Nobody used it in production.

Here's what we learned:

- Senders don't want recipients to linger. They want to know if they looked at all.
- The value isn't in the reading experience for recipients. It's in the analytics for senders.
- Features that seem smart in isolation often solve problems users don't actually have.

We removed the feature entirely. Product got simpler. NPS went up.

The lesson: when a feature has a low activation rate despite positive demo feedback, that's a signal mismatch — not a messaging problem. The use case doesn't exist yet.

Don't polish what people aren't using. Ship what's already working.
