## Proposed response

---

The timing here is good — DocSend killing the free plan left a real gap, and a lot of founders are evaluating alternatives right now.

One thing worth thinking through on pricing structure: the per-link model hits a pain point fast. A founder doing real fundraising sends the same deck to 40-60 VCs across 2-3 versions — that's not a "free tier" use case, and it's also not the enterprise budget. The sweet spot for alternatives to DocSend is probably a flat monthly fee rather than per-link, otherwise the economics start to feel similar at volume.

Curious whether you're handling the "VC downloads the PDF and shares it internally" case — most of the per-slide analytics tools lose all signal at that point. Does Pitchwise do anything to either reduce leakage or at least flag when a download event happens vs. a link view?

The other gap I'd look at: once a founder is beyond the "did they open it?" question and into "they've been back three times, what are they actually confused about?" — is there anything on the Q&A side? That's where the next layer of value in this category seems to live.
