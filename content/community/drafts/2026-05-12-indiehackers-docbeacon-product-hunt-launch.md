## Proposed response

---

Congrats on shipping — launch day is always a mix of exciting and terrifying.

The positioning observation you made is the real insight here. "Document tracking" sounds surveillance-y to half the people who hear it, but "know when to follow up" clicks immediately. The frame that worked for us was anchoring the value in a specific anxious moment rather than a feature description:

> "You sent the deck Thursday. It's Monday. Do you reach out or wait?"

That pause of uncertainty is something every founder and sales rep has felt. Lead with the moment, not the mechanism.

A few things that helped our PH launch on the conversion side:
- **Hunter selection matters more than timing.** Find someone with a genuinely engaged following in your target segment (founders, sales), not just a high follower count.
- **First comment is yours to set.** Post a genuine "here's the problem I kept running into" story as your first comment within minutes of going live — it seeds the conversation.
- **Reply to every single comment in the first 3 hours.** The algorithm rewards early comment velocity, and it signals to voters that a real person is behind it.

We've been building in the same space at Peeeky — happy to swap notes on what's resonated with your audience vs. ours. Always more useful to learn from someone solving the same problem differently than from generic PH advice.

Good luck today.

— Alex, Peeeky
