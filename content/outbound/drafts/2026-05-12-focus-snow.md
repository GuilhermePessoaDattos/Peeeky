You literally wrote the book on making M&A accessible — so I suspect you have strong opinions about the tools that make deals unnecessarily painful.

Traditional data rooms (Intralinks, Datasite) charge $15–50k per deal and still require buyers to navigate logins that weren't designed with ease of use in mind. For boutique deal processes, the setup cost often exceeds the value.

We built Peeeky as a simpler alternative: secure document sharing with NDA-gated access, page-level engagement tracking so you can see which buyers are doing real diligence, and an AI chat layer so parties can ask questions about documents without clogging your inbox. Up in hours, not days.
